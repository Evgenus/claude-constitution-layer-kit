# Module-document policy

Governs the document kind *module document*: the `MODULE.md` at the root of a module, which is the module's description from its first draft on (decision [dec-0003-module-spec-kind](../decisions/dec-0003-module-spec-kind.md)). Territory under this policy's id in `constitution.yaml`.

## Rules

1. <a id="r1"></a>**Shape.** Every section of `module-template/MODULE.md` filled, or "none" with one line of reasoning.
2. <a id="r2"></a>**Scope.** Cites exactly its matrix rows ([law 4 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-4)).
3. <a id="r3"></a>**Portability.** Carries no project reference ([law 2 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-2)). The kit is no project: the document stays in the module's folder, beside the plugin, and is never installed with it, and is read in the kit, so its links to kit documents are the description's own, and a count observed in the kit-live proof is evidence under rule 10; the portability grep of [requirement A12.1](../requirements.md#a12-1) holds the installed components, which reference nothing in the kit.
4. <a id="r4"></a>**Draft state.** Open slots are inline `[decide: …]` markers while the manifest version is `0.0.x`; a published module carries none. A `0.0.x` module is a draft and is not offered for installation; loading it from the kit's path for its live proofs is the one exception.
5. <a id="r5"></a>**Envelope.** `current-state`, `active`, superseded in place ([kernel §1](../constitution/CONVENTIONS.md#k1)); the id is `module-<plugin name>`, the form the territory `modules/*/MODULE.md` yields; the title is `const-<name> — <one clause saying what the module guards or does>`; the preamble's first sentence states the manifest version.
6. <a id="r6"></a>**Change.** The document changes with its code: a change under the module's pair of the kit's coverage map updates it or declares no impact, held by module [`const-task-close`](../requirements.md#const-task-close); a change to a rule the document states is `process` stakes ([law 9 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-9)), and any other edit is routine; a version bump edits the version sentence of the preamble, which after publication reads "Published at manifest version `x.y.z`."
7. <a id="r7"></a>**Anchors.** `t1` to `t13`, as the template numbers its sections; a section is added by a change to the template, never to one document.
8. <a id="r8"></a>**Handles.** One prefix per kind of handle in a document, never reused for another kind in the same document. A prefix is a letter or a word followed by a number, `T1`; a letter followed by a hyphen and a lowercase letter, `R-a`, is another prefix, the advisory rules'; a bare number is local to the list it stands in and is cited with its section, "limit 4 of the enforcement section"; an item added beside another takes that item's number with a letter, `L1b`; the rows of the artifacts table are keyed by their component and path. The kinds: the message keys as the module's `messages.py` names them; `E<n>` for the enforcement rows; the acceptance items numbered from 1 for the module's own and from 101 for the shared items, bare or under `T<n>` and `S<n>`; the live, eval, review and kit-check items continuing the module's own numbering or under `L<n>` — `V<n>` where `L` is the module's message prefix. A key of another document is written qualified.
9. <a id="r9"></a>**Shared content.** What the shared gate code carries — its messages, its limits, its acceptance items — enters by link, never restated: its messages to [kit-shared-messages](../../shared/MESSAGES.md#g3), its limits to [kit-shared-gate-code](../../shared/GATE.md#g4), its acceptance items to [kit-shared-items](../../shared/ITEMS.md#g5); the module's own messages are written out word for word ([rule 1 of plc-0009-shared-code](plc-0009-shared-code.md#r1)).
10. <a id="r10"></a>**Evidence.** A live proof carries its date, its environment and the platform's version; a proof deferred to the consumer's bootstrap is labelled `live, deferred` and links the step of the rollout plan that holds it; a platform fact carries its source — a page of the official documentation, the module's research comparison, or an observation on a named version of the platform — the date it was verified and what breaks if it changes; an `eval` item carries none of these in its row, its module's receipt in `scripts/eval-receipts.json` holding the date of its last attempt, the platform's version and the model. A live proof or a platform fact, which carries its date, may name the commit it was run or seen at ([requirement A5.6](../requirements.md#a5-6)).
11. <a id="r11"></a>**Files.** The document lists the module's files once, in its artifacts section; the template's plugin skeleton is not copied into it.
12. <a id="r12"></a>**Enforcement.** The envelope by module [`const-meta-types`](../requirements.md#const-meta-types); links and anchors by module [`const-referencing`](../requirements.md#const-referencing); the pairing with the code by module [`const-task-close`](../requirements.md#const-task-close) in its scan alone — the range check, run before a merge and on the trunk after it. That each acceptance item names the rows it proves in its `rows` cell, and each row the rows section holds is named by an item, is held by the kit's `scripts/check-aspects.py`, a line of the gate list ([kit-template-slot-guidance](../../module-template/slot-guidance.md#s3)); a count typed on the Runner line of section 10, `<digits> items green`, is held against the runner's total by the same script under `--run`. The form of the enforcement table of [kit-template-slot-guidance](../../module-template/slot-guidance.md#s6) — each row opening `E<n>`, bold or not, each `--scan` span in a row in a declared or a marked form, and no span in the declared form in the section's prose — and each declared scan standing in the gate list, in its module's CI job and in the install guide's scan block, together with the module's pair in the coverage map of the range check, are held by the kit's `scripts/check-gate-lists.py`, a line of the gate list. That no prefix of rule 8 serves two kinds of handle — the message keys, the enforcement rows and the acceptance items — and that a module's message table takes a key of the shared table only as [§3 of the shared gate code](../../shared/MESSAGES.md#g3a) lists, are held by the kit's `scripts/check-handle-forms.py`, a line of the gate list. Whether an item proves the rows it names, and the rows, shape, portability and handles otherwise, are review — the fresh-context validation of step 6 of [the module-development page](../guide/developing-a-module.md#s6). The twelve traces of the style defaults are held by module [`const-styling`](../requirements.md#const-styling) in its scan alone, which runs no hook. The grade, the sentence length and the constructions of the prose are held by module [`const-language`](../requirements.md#const-language), after a write and in its scan. The length in characters is held by module [`const-doc-budgets`](../requirements.md#const-doc-budgets), after a write and in its scan, against the ceiling its dial `ceilings` in `constitution.yaml` sets for documents of this kind.
13. <a id="r13"></a>**Style.** Documents of this kind follow the style guides the dial `guides` of module [`const-styling`](../requirements.md#const-styling) names in `constitution.yaml`, and depart from a default only where that module's dial `departures` names a departure for their territory, with its reason ([rule 9 of plc-0001-root-policy](plc-0001-root-policy.md#r9)); the traces of the defaults are held as the enforcement rule says, the rest is review.
