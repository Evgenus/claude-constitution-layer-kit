# Intent policy

Governs the document kind *intent page*: the frame every design and edit of the kit is checked against. Territory under this policy's id in `constitution.yaml`.

## Rules

1. **Type.** `current-state`, superseded in place ([kernel §1](../../kernel/CONVENTIONS.md#k1)).
2. **Change.** A change to the frame is an explicit `process`-stakes decision, never a side effect of other work ([law 9 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-9)).
3. **Content.** States what the kit is, what it is not, and its design principles; it holds no rule that belongs to the kernel and no procedure that belongs to a module.
