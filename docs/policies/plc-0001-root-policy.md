# Root policy

Governs the document kind *policy*: a document that states the rules of one document kind, this one included. Territory under this policy's id in `constitution.yaml`.

This document is the root of governance in this repository, per [kernel §3](../../kernel/CONVENTIONS.md#k3): its territory, declared under its id in the kit's `constitution.yaml`, is where policies live, and this file lies inside it. A document is a policy because it sits here. Nothing declares itself one.

## Rules

1. <a id="r1"></a>**Home.** Every policy of the kit is a Markdown document in this policy's territory, carrying the kernel envelope ([kernel §1](../../kernel/CONVENTIONS.md#k1)) with `type: current-state`.
2. <a id="r2"></a>**Territory.** A policy claims paths only through its dials namespace, keyed by its id, in `constitution.yaml`. A policy with no namespace claims nothing.
3. <a id="r3"></a>**Creation and change.** Adding, removing, or re-scoping a policy, and amending a rule of one, is a `process`-stakes decision ([kernel §4](../../kernel/CONVENTIONS.md#k4)): a record in `docs/decisions/` and one human yes. Carrying an already-ruled item into a policy, its ruling unchanged, is a repair and routine ([rule 1 of plc-0004-decision-records](plc-0004-decision-records.md#r1)); a mechanical edit — a typo, a reference-form migration — is routine too. When module `const-locations` is installed, its creation command writes the document here — from the policy template when `const-locations.templates` names one for this policy, with the envelope alone otherwise; the body follows the template's shape either way. Until then, by hand from the template. The template is `templates/policy.md`; a rule of this policy that changes the shape changes the template in the same change.
4. <a id="r4"></a>**Body.** A policy's body is prose: what kind it governs, its rules as numbered items, and the kernel sections it relies on by link. It opens with the sentence the template writes — "Governs the document kind *<kind>*: …" — its title is "<Kind> policy — <one clause>", the kind named in full or by its short name — "Intent policy" for the intent page, and "Root policy — <one clause>" for this policy, whose kind is *policy* — and every rule opens with a bold name. It never restates the kernel ([law 1 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-1)) and never lists the documents it governs — the territory under its id is the enumeration.
5. <a id="r5"></a>**One kind.** A policy governs exactly one document kind ([kernel §3](../../kernel/CONVENTIONS.md#k3)). A document of a kind no policy governs gets its policy first, by rule 3.
6. <a id="r6"></a>**Number.** Policies are a numbered kind: id and filename stem are one name, `plc-<nnnn>-<slug>`, the slug naming the kind governed and never the kit; prefix, width and territory are an entry of `const-catalogs.catalogs` in `constitution.yaml`, its territory this policy's own by a YAML alias. A new policy takes one more than the highest number the catalog has held, deleted policies included, provisional until it lands on main ([requirement A13.1](../requirements.md#a13-1), [A13.3](../requirements.md#a13-3)). A policy's id is also its namespace key in `constitution.yaml` (rule 2), so the key is written in the same form; a `plc-` name written outside Markdown — a key, the `root_policy` path, `record_policies` — matches a pattern of the `mentions` dial of module `const-referencing`, whose scan reports one that is not an id a document the scan covers carries.
7. <a id="r7"></a>**Anchors.** Every rule carries an explicit anchor `r<n>` inside its item, before the bold name, as the laws of `CLAUDE.md` do ([requirement A5.5](../requirements.md#a5-5)). From another document a rule is cited as a link to its anchor, "rule n of <policy id>" being the link's text or the words that carry it; inside its own policy, "rule n" is enough. A rule keeps its number and anchor for life, since accepted records cite rules by number and are not edited to fit later text: a rule withdrawn keeps its number and its item says so, with a link to the record that withdrew it; a new rule takes the next number.
8. <a id="r8"></a>**Enforcement.** Every policy carries a rule so named, stating which module guards which field of its kind — at write time, in its scan, or both, the split named where the two differ — and what stays judgment, held by review ([law 5 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-5)). For the kind this policy governs: the envelope by module `const-meta-types`; the name, the id and a new item's number, free in the working tree, by module `const-catalogs` at write time and in its scan, and every collision between items that exist in the tree it scans, in its scan alone; links and anchors by module `const-referencing`, and the `plc-` mentions outside Markdown by it in its scan alone; whether a body states one kind's rules, and whether a rule is stated in full, is review.
