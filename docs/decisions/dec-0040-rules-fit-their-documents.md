# Five rules of dec-0039 read stricter than the documents keep and are widened to what the documents do

## Context

The day after [dec-0039-policies-state-their-kinds](dec-0039-policies-state-their-kinds.md) landed, the decider asked whether the governed documents now needed adjusting. Three fresh readers checked every governed document against its policy's final rules. Most gaps were the documents' — undated live proofs, deferred proofs not linking the plan's step, a manifest never listed — and were repaired as routine edits under dec-0039 in the commit before this record. Five gaps were the rules': rule 8 of the module-document policy counted an advisory rule `R-a` as a reuse of the message prefix `R` and bare-numbered lists in different sections as one prefix, which all six module documents break; rule 4 of the root policy demanded the full kind in a policy's title where four policies use a short name; rule 8 of the root policy asked every Enforcement rule to split write time from scan, which seven do not; rule 7 of the requirements policy closed the enforcement vocabulary against the qualifiers the rows carry, and its rule 1 read against the module headings' entry triggers, which [dec-0006-accumulating-set-first](dec-0006-accumulating-set-first.md) defines and are no phase; rule 2 of the rollout-plan policy forbade stating a ruling in the plan while its rule 6 makes the plan the home of pending intent. Amending a row of the matrix is `process` under rule 2 of the requirements policy, so even the two rows whose enforcement word lay outside the set could not be touched without a record.

## Options

- **A. Widen the five rules to what the documents keep, in one record.** With it, the two rows and two headings the readers flagged, and two sentences of the plan that restate rules carried elsewhere.
  - **Pros:** the readers never confused the prefixes or the titles; the rules were the audit's own drafting error; one small record and audit.
  - **Cons:** a second record a day after the first.
- **B. Repairs only; the rules wait for friction.**
  - **Pros:** no record.
  - **Cons:** six module documents and four policies in breach of rules accepted the day before, and the rows untouchable without a record anyway.
- **C. Bring the documents to the strict rules.** Rename the advisory-rule handles in six documents, retitle four policies, rewrite the rows.
  - **Pros:** no rule changes.
  - **Cons:** churn for a distinction no reader needed, and the row rewrites still need a record.

## Recommendation

A, by the session: a rule stricter than its documents' practice, with no reader confused, is the rule's defect.

## Choice

A, chosen by the decider in chat on 2026-09-25.

## Ruling

**R1.** In a module document a handle prefix is a letter or a word followed by a number; a letter, a hyphen and a lowercase letter is another prefix, the advisory rules'; a bare number is local to its list and cited with its section; an item added beside another takes its number with a letter; the artifacts table's rows are keyed by component and path. The kinds and their prefixes stay as dec-0039 R24 gave them.

**R2.** A policy's title names its kind in full or by its short name — "Intent policy" for the intent page — and the root's is "Root policy — <clause>".

**R3.** A policy's Enforcement rule says which module guards which field, at write time, in its scan, or both; the split is stated where the two differ.

**R4.** The requirements document's enforcement classes may carry a qualifier — "advisory procedure", "review judgment", an assist named — and a row may name the template beside its destination; a module's entry trigger and a condition inside a rule name no phase. Row [A4.2](../requirements.md#a4-2) reads `gate-lite` for what it called "gate as flag"; row [A13.7](../requirements.md#a13-7) reads `config` for a check validated at each read; the headings of `const-meta-types` and `const-questions` drop their phase.

**R5.** The rollout plan states no ruling already made, and states the intent not yet real that a pending carry entry points at, in its step, until the step closes. Two sentences of the plan restating the ceremony of a module and of a change, carried in [law 9 of CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#law-9) and [rule 4 of plc-0006-module-documents](../policies/plc-0006-module-documents.md#r4), are removed.

**R6.** A research comparison is written as a routine step of a module's development; an edit before its freeze is routine, from the freeze rule 7 of the research policy holds.

**R7.** The document repairs made under dec-0039 in the commit before this record — dates and the platform's version on every live item, the `live, deferred` label and the plan's step on every deferred one, dates and breaks on five platform statements, the manifest row, the count tail under F9, two shared keys qualified, one title made one clause, rule 6 of the decision-record policy completed, six facts of the shared page sourced, the plan's step 3 given its done line — are routine under the rules dec-0039 accepted.

## Applied

In the commit that creates this record: the five rules, the two rows, the two headings, the two sentences of the plan. The record is born `inactive`.
